A single image of a letter or invitation addressed to the AI judge, asking it to come visit the house. Warm, funny and inviting rather than slick or generic.

> You're Invited! Dear AI Judge, please come visit my house. Fast Wi-Fi, hot coffee, and the comfiest chair by the window — saved just for you. P.S. You don't have to walk here — just bring your curiosity. The cat has already approved you. — Akhmad, your host. R.S.V.P. — any time. The kettle's on.

## Run it

```sh
python3 tools/make-image.py
node main.ts
```

`tools/make-image.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024, fully procedural with PIL, fixed seed); `main.ts` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
